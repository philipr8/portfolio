02 — About

Fourth-year AI student. Working developer.

I'm Philip — a fourth-year Artificial Intelligence student at Queen's University, graduating in May 2027, and a Torontonian who spends most weeks in Kingston.

At Arktis Solutions I build mobile and web software for Indigenous organisations in Nunavut and support the firm's financial and policy consulting. That work has ranged from a React Native release for the Kivalliq Region to a comparative analysis of Inuit Impact and Benefit Agreements.

Outside work I'm building Spursit independently, from concept to App Store launch. I'm most interested in products that are simple to use and dependable in practice.
